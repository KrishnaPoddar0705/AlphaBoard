# Company Brain — landing loop

- **Deliverable:** 12 s silent loop, 1600×1000, 30 fps, H.264 → `landing/public/media/brain-film.mp4` + poster.
- **Subject:** AlphaBoard's Company Brain: a question to the firm's research memory, answered with cited sources, then filed to the company's record.
- **Look:** dark room. Ground #0b0b0c, surface #141416, ink #f2f2f0, muted #a1a1a6, accent gold #e6c47e once (the source count). Panchang 700 caps for display, Switzer 400/500 for text. Glass cards: rgba(255,255,255,0.06), top-edge highlight, backdrop blur, one specular sweep. No glow, no gradient grounds.
- **Hero object:** the user's own crystal clip (`assets/crystal.mp4`, from glass3.mp4), screen-blended at centre.
- **Beats:** 0–2.5 question types · 2.5–6 five source cards settle round the crystal · 6–9 cited answer assembles · 9–12 cards collapse into the "filed to the record" line, end card, fade to ground for the loop.
- **Content rule:** fictional companies only (Deccan Auto Components). No real tickers.
- **Audio:** none.
